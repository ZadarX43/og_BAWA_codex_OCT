# Homepage Consumer Product Upgrade Audit

Date: 2026-05-28

## Summary

Odds Genius already has a strong dark intelligence-system identity, but the homepage can still feel like an internal command dashboard rather than a finished consumer product. The next design move is to keep the seriousness of the system while hiding complexity behind a clearer, calmer customer journey.

The target feel is:

- FPL polish
- Bloomberg Terminal intelligence
- Stripe-style SaaS clarity
- elite football scouting room

## Core Principle

Hide complexity behind confidence. Show the user the power, but do not make them carry the cognitive load of the system.

## Main Problems To Fix

- Do not lead with `Football v0.1.2`; versioning belongs in footer, changelog, dashboard, or methodology.
- Reduce proof density above the fold.
- Replace internal labels with customer-facing product language.
- Add one large product-preview visual near the top so users immediately understand what they get.
- Reduce nested card depth.
- Make CTAs more decisive.
- Give sections more breathing room.

## Homepage Flow

1. Hero: explain Odds Genius in one sentence.
2. Live Prediction Preview: show the user what a fixture read looks like.
3. Proof Strip: show only the strongest headline proof stats.
4. Three Core Cards: explain the main product actions.
5. Today's Prediction Desk: show live usefulness.
6. Membership Access: convert interested users.
7. Verified Performance Layer: show deeper proof.
8. How Odds Genius Works: explain methodology calmly.
9. Responsible Decision Layer: show discipline and risk awareness.

## Language Map

- Founder proof layer -> Verified Performance Layer
- Models -> Prediction Markets
- Player events -> Player Intelligence
- Tested outcome viability -> Live Model Health
- World Cup + pre-season edition -> World Cup Intelligence Pack
- How to read it -> How Odds Genius Works
- Today on the desk -> Today's Prediction Desk
- No edge is also information -> Disciplined by Design
- Decision layer -> Decision Confidence

## Hero Direction

Eyebrow:

`ODDS GENIUS FOOTBALL INTELLIGENCE`

Headline:

`Find the strongest football prediction edges before kick-off.`

Subcopy:

`Model-led match intelligence across FTR, goals, BTTS, Correct Score and player-event markets.`

Primary CTA:

`Open prediction desk`

Secondary CTA:

`View proof layer`

Founder CTA:

`Join Founder Access`

## Product Preview Direction

The hero should include a large prediction card preview with:

- fixture
- market
- customer state: deploy / watch / avoid
- model probability
- bookmaker implied probability
- edge
- reasoning tokens
- CTA to view match intelligence

This answers the user's first question: "What do I actually get?"

## Design Rules

- Maximum visible card depth: one.
- Use fewer, larger panels.
- Keep text short in the first viewport.
- Let proof build trust, but do not make the user decode the whole model estate immediately.
- Use football-native cues: team names, fixture rows, kick-off times, market labels, confidence states, and pitch/data motifs.
- The homepage should feel alive: prediction desk, market watchlist, injury warnings, World Cup preparation, and no-bet discipline.
